Project 1 - Form Generator

Description:

A scene inspired by the ending of a game called "Z.A.T.O. // I Love the World and Everything In It". This project depicts a radio tower in a gloomy sky with upwards falling snow, which can be zoomed out to brighten the sky into sunrise colors, and to transmit a message.

Instructions:

Drag the mouse side to side to zoom in/out and change the color of the sky. Leave the scene fully zoomed out for a short time to display a message
